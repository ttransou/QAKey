# QAKey Use Cases

## Start here

QAKey fits teams that want approved answers maintained by people and delivered deterministically to users.
Use this guide to decide whether QAKey matches your workflow.

---

## Small nonprofit or small team

QAKey works well when one person or a small group owns the knowledge base.

- Publish a small FAQ for staffing, hours, donations, or basic policy questions
- Let a non-developer maintain answers in the editor
- Keep Draft records private until reviewed
- Use Active records for answers users should receive immediately

Best fit when:

- You need a simple, trusted answer layer
- The same people who answer questions can maintain the content
- You want to avoid prompt tuning or AI platform overhead

---

## Internal support desk

QAKey is a strong fit for internal help desks that answer repeated process questions.

- Store canonical questions for common requests
- Add alternate phrasings that employees actually use
- Use reviewer fields to track who approved the answer
- Sunset old guidance instead of rewriting history

Best fit when:

- Answers must stay consistent across staff and channels
- Knowledge updates should be fast and auditable
- Support teams need to reduce repeated questions

---

## Larger governed deployment

QAKey can serve as a controlled answer layer inside a broader knowledge architecture.

- Keep policy owners responsible for content
- Let engineering manage deployment and integration
- Use the REST API for portals, bots, and internal tools
- Treat Active records as the only live answer set

Best fit when:

- You want deterministic answers with clear ownership
- You need a lightweight governance model
- You prefer a system that is easy to audit and explain

---

## When QAKey is a good fit

- Approved answers should be returned verbatim
- Maintainers should own the wording
- You want predictable behavior over generative responses

## When QAKey is not a good fit

- You need open-ended AI-generated answers
- No one owns the source content
- You want an autonomous policy decision-maker
